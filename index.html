ScanMyRoom checks a room for hidden cameras using the sensors in your iPhone, and says plainly what each technique can and cannot see.

Common questions

  • The Wi-Fi scan found nothing. Check that Local Network access is on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Some hotel and office networks also isolate guests from each other, which hides every device.
  • The lens scan will not start. It needs a dim room: in bright light the flash cannot be told apart from the room, so the scan declines to guess.
  • Does the infrared scan work on my phone? Point a TV remote at the camera and press a button. If the LED shows on screen, it does.
  • Subscriptions. Manage or cancel in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Every detection technique is free without one.
