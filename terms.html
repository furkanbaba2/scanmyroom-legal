1. What ScanMyRoom is

ScanMyRoom helps you look for hidden cameras and recording devices using the sensors in your iPhone and a guided physical search. It reports what those sensors can and cannot establish, and grades every technique by how much it can actually be trusted.

2. What ScanMyRoom is not — read this part

The app cannot prove that a room is clean, and it never claims to.

There are devices it is incapable of finding. A camera that records to a memory card and transmits nothing emits no signal any phone sensor can read. A device running on its own hidden network does not appear on the network you are connected to, because iOS does not let any app list nearby Wi-Fi networks. A microphone that only listens has no lens, no emitter and no radio, and no phone sensor of any kind can detect it — this is a matter of physics, not of software quality.

A scan that finds nothing means the scans found nothing. It does not mean there is nothing there.

3. Reports are records, not certificates

A saved report is a record of what was checked, when, what was found, and explicitly what the check could not cover. It is not a certification that any space is free of surveillance devices, and must not be presented as one.

4. Lawful use

You may use ScanMyRoom only where you are lawfully entitled to do so. It is for checking spaces you occupy or are responsible for — your home, a hotel room you have booked, a rental you are staying in, a workplace you are permitted to inspect.

You may not use it to access, interfere with or attempt to gain entry to networks or devices you have no right to. The app identifies devices; it never attempts to authenticate to them, and you should not either.

5. If you find something

Do not dismantle or unplug it. Photograph it where it sits. If you believe you have been recorded without consent, that is a criminal matter in most countries and the scene is evidence. Nothing in this app is legal advice.
